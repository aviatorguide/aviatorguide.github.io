Chicken Road casino game: the crossing in plain words

Chicken Road is a crossing game from InOut Games: you move a chicken across a road lane by lane, each lane passed raises the multiplier, and you can cash out after any lane. If the chicken is hit, the stake is lost. It belongs to the crash games in Ethiopia that I cover, and you open it through a licensed operator's lobby once you are 21 or older.

Chicken Road or Chicken Road 2?

Many searches ask for Chicken Road 2. The studio released Chicken Road 2.0 on 15 April 2025 with an RTP of 95.5%, lower than the 98% of the original. This page stays on the original game, so look at the exact title in the lobby before you play.

How it compares, and who it suits

The closest cousin on this site is Tower Rush, the floor-by-floor game: both wait for you between steps and let you stop at any moment. The difference is the choice of difficulty in Chicken Road, which you set before crossing. It suits players who like a slow, step by step decision far more than a fast multiplier race.

Chicken Road strategy: level first, exit lane second

The only Chicken Road strategy that holds up is a stopping rule: pick your level and your exit lane before the first step, then keep to them. No plan changes the chance of the chicken being hit on the next lane. What it changes is how much you risk and when you walk away.

  1. Choose the difficulty: easy, medium, hard or hardcore.
  2. Set the stake before the first lane, inside your session budget.
  3. Move the chicken one lane and watch the multiplier rise.
  4. After each lane, decide: cross again or cash out.
  5. Cash out to keep the win; if the chicken is hit, the bet is lost.

How to cash out in Chicken Road

The cash out button is active after every lane you pass. For example, with a 20 birr stake and a multiplier of x2.5 on screen, cashing out returns 50 birr to your balance. One more lane can raise that number or end the crossing, and the game does not warn you which.

A good Chicken Road strategy also covers the session, not only the crossing. I decide how many crossings I play and what loss ends the day.

  • There is no "best time to play": every crossing is random and ignores the last one.
  • Keep the same level for a whole session instead of jumping up after a loss.
  • Do not double the stake to recover; the road does not owe you a win.
  • Remember the 98% RTP is a long run average, not a promise for tonight.
  • Stop once your budget is spent, and come back another day.

My note. I write my exit lane for each level on a small card. Without it, I always want "just one more lane".

How Chicken Road makes its results

Chicken Road works on a random draw made by the game server. Each crossing ignores the ones before, and the rules show the 98% RTP of the original version. I have not found a provably fair check published for this game, so I do not claim one: what you can do is read the rules and the RTP inside the game.

A Chicken Road hack would need to read the server before the result exists. That is why every seller asks for something first, money or your login.

  • A fee before you see a single "prediction".
  • A request for your password, SMS code or wallet PIN.
  • A file shared through a chat link instead of an official store.
  • Screenshots of wins only, never full results.
  • Words like "guaranteed", "100%" or "secret bug".

My own routine is simple. I learn the levels in the demo, set my budget, and treat every crossing as a fresh random result, whatever a tool claims.
