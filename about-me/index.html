About me: why I write Entoto Skies

Who writes Entoto Skies

I am Hanna, and that is a pen name. I am a player from Addis Ababa who got curious about how crash games really work, and Entoto Skies is where I write down what I learn. The name comes from the Entoto hills above the city, where the sky opens up like the screen of Aviator when the plane starts to climb.

I have no diploma in mathematics, no job at a studio and no award to show you. What I do have is a method: I read the rules of each game, I test it in the free demo, and I only write figures that the studios publish themselves.

Why this site exists

Most pages I found about these games either shouted about big wins or sold fake predictors. I wanted one calm place, in plain English, that explains the rounds, the real odds and the way birr moves in and out of an account. Every guide here follows the same idea: try the demo first, set a budget, and stop when it is spent.

The site is for adults aged 21 and over. I never write about personal wins, because one lucky evening proves nothing and would only push you to chase the same thing.

How the site earns money

Entoto Skies is not an operator. I take no bets and hold no deposits, and I cannot open, fix or close an account for you. The "Play" button on my pages leads to a partner offer, and if you sign up through it, the partner may pay me a commission.

That commission never changes what I write about a game, its odds or its weak points. If a game has something I dislike, you will read it on its page, partner or not.
