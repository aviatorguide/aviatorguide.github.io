Penalty Duel strategy: choosing kicker or keeper

A Penalty Duel strategy is mostly a choice of role and stake, because the shot itself is random. The kicker pays from x2 to x15, the keeper from x1.2 to x2.5 with lower volatility. Pick the side that fits your budget, keep a flat stake, and stop at a limit you set before the first shot.

  1. Choose your role: kicker or goalkeeper.
  2. Set the stake before the shot, within your session budget.
  3. Take the shot or try the save.
  4. Read the result: kicker x2 to x15, keeper x1.2 to x2.5.
  5. Switch role, play the next shot or stop.

Chance x5, Buy Bonus and autoplay

The Chance x5 feature brings the golden ball or the golden gloves, and the rules show what it costs and changes. Buy Bonus opens a bonus chain of multipliers up to x4,860, but whether you see it depends on the operator. Autoplay is there too; I set a loss limit before I ever turn it on.

For example, a 20 birr stake as keeper at x1.5 returns 30 birr, while the same stake as kicker at x4 returns 80 birr. The kicker's bigger number comes with more misses, which is what medium volatility means in practice.

  • Stay in one role for a block of shots instead of switching after every loss.
  • Keep the stake flat; raising it to recover is how budgets vanish.
  • Remember the 96.14% RTP is an average over a very long run.
  • Read the Chance x5 and Buy Bonus terms before using either.
  • End the session at your loss limit, with no extra "last shot".
