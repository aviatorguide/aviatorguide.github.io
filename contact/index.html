Contact: how questions are handled here

How to reach me

For now, Entoto Skies has no public contact channel. There is no e-mail address, no contact form and no chat on this site, and I do not answer through social media pages that use the site's name. If a page or profile claims to be me and asks for money or your details, it is not me.

I know that sounds strict. A small site written by one player cannot offer real support, and I would rather say that clearly than leave a mailbox nobody reads.

Account, deposit or withdrawal problems

I am not an operator, so I cannot see your account, your balance or your payments. Questions about login, verification, a deposit through telebirr or another wallet, a withdrawal or a bonus can only be solved by the operator where you play. Use the support section inside the operator's site or app, and keep your transaction reference and SMS ready when you write to them.

Never share your password or a confirmation code with anyone who contacts you first. A real support team never needs them.

Where to find answers on this site

Many questions already have an answer in the questions readers ask me most on the home page. Each game guide also explains login, the free demo, the odds and birr payments for that game.

If the game no longer feels like fun, please read my page on playing safe. It lists the warning signs and the tools that help you take a break.
