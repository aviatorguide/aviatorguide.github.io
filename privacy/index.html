Privacy: what Entoto Skies does with your data

What this site collects

Entoto Skies collects as little as possible. There are no user accounts, no newsletter, no comments and no contact form, so I never ask for your name, phone number or e-mail. The site uses no analytics service and no tracking or advertising cookies.

The free demos on the game pages load only when you click to start them. At that moment the game comes from its studio, which applies its own rules to that session.

Server logs

Like almost every website, the server that hosts Entoto Skies keeps standard technical logs. They contain things like your IP address, the browser type, the page requested and the time of the visit. These logs are used only to keep the site working and safe, and they are kept for a limited time before they are deleted.

The Play button and the partner path

The "Play" button leads to a path on this site that starts with /go/, which then forwards you to the partner offer. On the way, a UTM tag is added to the address with an identifier of the page you came from, so the partner can tell which guide sent the visit. No personal data is added to that tag.

Once you are on the partner's site, its own privacy policy applies. Read it before you sign up, because that is where your account and payment data are handled.

Your rights

Since I hold no account or contact data about you, there is very little I could show, correct or delete. You can block cookies and clear your browser history at any time, and you can refuse the partner's cookies on its own site. There is no e-mail address for privacy requests, because there is nothing personal stored here to request.
